Violet’s Cookie Recipe


Ingredients

→ 125g Unsalted butter (unmelted)

→ 50g granulated sugar

→ 200g Light brown sugar

→ 1 large egg + 1 yolk

→ 1 tsp vanilla extract


→ ½ tsp kosher salt

→ ½ tsp baking soda

→ 200g all purpose flower


→ 150 g chocolate mixins

→ 1 tsb instant coffee fine (optional)


Preparation

  1. To prepare these cookies you must first prepare your mind.

  2. First melt the butter in a saucepan, on low let it slowly simmer till its golden with a slightly nutty smell

  3. mix the sugars in

  4. once it has cooled slightly mix in the eggs and vanilla

  5. in another bowl add salt, baking soda, and flour, mix

  6. mix wet and try together

  7. chop up your chocolate, do lots of different sizes, makes it nice.

  8. Rest the dough in the fridge for a day

  9. set the oven to 180C fan forced

  10. make egg sized balls of dough and place em on a baking paper lined tray and cook for 12 min

  11. from that point every 5 mins take em from the oven and drop the tray from about 10cm in the air a few times, nocking the air out of them, this makes them so chewy and yummy.

  12. keep doing this till they are golden brown. then take them out and sprinkle with flakey seasalt.

  13. Let cool, they r pretty much toffee when they r hot so like if you touch them they will like 3rd degree burn you

  14. enjoy :]
